Original PNGs are retained without distortion or recoloring. Logos identify their respective leagues; Sports Lab remains independent. Soccer uses the existing football symbol because that workspace includes multiple leagues.

Player headshots use the supplied NFL/ESPN/MLB provider image or a player ID in the matching provider's namespace. MLB uses its transparent `headshot/silo/current` PNG cutouts instead of the gray-backed `/67` portrait. Team badges use the current profile's team or ESPN team ID. These remote assets are not included in this folder.

Team badges overlap the lower shoulder of the player cutout and retain transparent backgrounds. MLB artwork is selected per team after comparing standard and alternate marks over actual headshots at row and detail sizes. Dark MLB crests use ESPN's brighter `500-dark` variants; primary artwork remains for already-legible marks such as Philadelphia, Texas, San Francisco and Baltimore. NFL retains ESPN's `500-dark` artwork. Alternate assets fall back to the original `500` URL if unavailable.

Two official MLB SVGs are retained unchanged in `../teams/mlb/` (retrieved September 23, 2026):

- `nym.svg`: https://www.mlbstatic.com/team-logos/team-cap-on-light/121.svg — solid orange NY cap mark, replacing the dark navy version.
- `mia.svg`: https://www.mlbstatic.com/team-logos/team-cap-on-dark/146.svg — cap mark with a stronger light outline than the ESPN version.
