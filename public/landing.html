DATA, WITHOUT THE GUESSWORK

Know what you're looking at.

Every result needs a sample, a timestamp, and a source. Sportslab keeps those details beside the chart.

Open the full workspace ↗
01Where do these game results come from?+

The NFL receiving yard results on this page come from completed games in the nflverse weekly player statistics. The research panel links to the source files and shows the latest game date in the selected sample.

02Are the comparison lines live?+

No. The page uses a captured public sportsbook comparison when one is available. Its book, capture time, and stale status appear with the research. Moving the line is your own what-if comparison.

03Does a hit rate predict the next game?+

No. It counts results above or below the selected line in completed games. It is not a probability for the next game. The full workspace separates experimental forecasts from historical results.

04Where are my picks and notes saved?+

Saved picks and notes in the workspace use this browser's local storage. The homepage research controls are temporary and reset when the page reloads.
