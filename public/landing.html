Before you get started.

Is this demo showing real picks?

No. The demo uses fictional players and sample games so you can try every control. Open the research workspace for available source data, posted lines and their limitations.

Where are my picks and notes saved?

Your records in the research workspace stay in this browser. The homepage demo is separate: its saves and notes last only until you reload.

Does a hit rate predict the next game?

No. A hit rate describes the games in the selected sample. Model estimates are shown separately, are experimental, and can be unavailable when the required data is missing.
