Questions

Straight answers.

Still curious? The help center covers every tool in detail.

Visit the help centerGuides for every tool, market and calculator→
01Does VisualOdds place bets for me?

No. VisualOdds is a research and tracking workspace. You compare prices and evidence here, then place any bet yourself at a licensed sportsbook.

02Where do the numbers come from?

Player history comes from public datasets and box scores, and prices come from connected feeds or entries you make. Every view shows its sample size, source and capture time so you can check it.

03What does positive EV mean?

A bet has positive expected value when its price pays more than the fair, no-vig probability implies. It is an estimate over many bets, not a promise that any single bet wins.

04Are projections guaranteed?

No. Projections and simulations are experimental estimates under stated assumptions. Historical hit rates describe the past; they are not future probabilities.

05Is the bet tracker free?

Yes. Every account includes the bet tracker and saved filters. Research, models and the market tools are part of the paid plans shown above.
